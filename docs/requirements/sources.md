## S1 — Joint FIP/WHO guidelines on good pharmacy practice

**Reference.** World Health Organization and International Pharmaceutical
Federation, "Joint FIP/WHO guidelines on good pharmacy practice: standards for
quality of pharmacy services," WHO Technical Report Series, No. 961, Annex 8,
2011. Record: <https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/annex-8-trs-961>.
Copy read: <https://fip.org/file/1476>.
**Read by:** Minh (C), 12/10/2026.

Role 1, Function F, "Dispose of medicine preparations and medical products":

> Pharmacists should ensure that regular monitoring of the medicines inventory
> is conducted and should always include medicines samples in the process of
> periodic inspection for expiration dates and removal of outdated stock.

> Pharmacists should ensure that recalled medical products, including
> medicines samples, are immediately stored separately for subsequent disposal
> and prevented from being available for further dispensing or distribution.

> Pharmacists should establish a safe way of medicines waste disposal at the
> hospital and/or community pharmacy so that patients and the public can be
> encouraged to return their expired or unwanted medicines and medical
> devices.

**Used for:** the problem description (stock must be monitored for expiry and
outdated stock removed); decision 4 (stock that comes back is kept apart and
cannot be dispensed until someone decides what happens to it); UC09, UC11.

## S2 — Vietnam Ministry of Health, Circular 02/2018/TT-BYT on good pharmacy practice, Annex II-2b

**Reference.** Bộ Y tế, Thông tư 02/2018/TT-BYT ngày 22/01/2018 quy định về
Thực hành tốt cơ sở bán lẻ thuốc, Phụ lục II-2b "Danh mục kiểm tra (checklist)
thực hành tốt cơ sở bán lẻ thuốc đối với quầy thuốc", consolidated text
including amendments by Circulars 12/2020/TT-BYT and 11/2025/TT-BYT. Copy read:
<https://files.thuvienphapluat.vn/uploads/DocForms/3/2/6/6/326672//Ph%E1%BB%A5%20l%E1%BB%A5c%20II-2b-02-2018-TT-BYT.doc>.
**Read by:** Minh (C), 12/10/2026.

This annex is the inspection checklist for a *quầy thuốc* (drug counter). The
checklist for a *nhà thuốc* (pharmacy) is a separate annex of the same circular
that we have not yet obtained; the items below are the ones we expect it to
share, and that has to be checked before the report relies on it.

| Item | Original text | Our translation |
|---|---|---|
| 5.3.1 | "Theo dõi các thông tin thuốc và các vấn đề có liên quan: – Theo dõi bằng máy tính – Theo dõi bằng sổ … – Có theo dõi đối với thuốc kê đơn, thuốc dạng phối hợp có chứa dược chất gây nghiện, dược chất hướng thần." | Medicine information is tracked, by computer or in a ledger, including tracking of prescription medicines and combination products containing narcotic or psychotropic substances. |
| 8.1 | "Có kiểm tra, kiểm soát khi nhập thuốc: – Hạn dùng của thuốc – Thuốc còn nguyên vẹn trong bao bì gốc của nhà sản xuất …" | Medicines are checked on receipt, including their expiry date and that they are intact in the manufacturer's packaging. |
| 9.1 | "Có tiếp nhận và lưu thông tin hoặc lưu các thông báo về thuốc khiếu nại, thuốc không được phép lưu hành, thuốc phải thu hồi" | Notices about medicines under complaint, medicines not permitted for sale and medicines to be recalled are received and kept. |
| 9.2 | "Có thu hồi và lập hồ sơ thu hồi theo quy định, Có kiểm kê đối với thuốc khiếu nại, thuốc phải thu hồi (Nếu đến kỳ kiểm kê thuốc thu hồi chưa được xử lý)." | Recalls are carried out and recorded; medicines under complaint or recall are included in the stock count if they have not been dealt with by the time of the count. Evidence the inspector checks: "biên bản kiểm kê, hồ sơ lưu" (stock-count minutes, records kept). |
| 9.4 | "Có trả lại nơi mua hoặc hủy" | Such medicines are returned to the supplier or destroyed. Evidence: records kept. |

**Used for:** the problem description (a pharmacy is inspected on whether it
tracks medicines, checks expiry on receipt and keeps stock-count records);
decision 4 (stock that cannot be sold is counted and then returned to the
supplier or destroyed, with records, rather than drifting back onto the
shelf); UC03 receipt checks; UC09 stock count records; UC11 write-off.
