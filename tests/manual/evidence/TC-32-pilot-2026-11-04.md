[`../tc32-task-sheet.md`](../tc32-task-sheet.md) on a seeded database. The
observer was Minh, who recorded the session and handed the notes to Duy Anh for

  prepared in advance as the observer sheet says.
- One participant from outside the team. The pilot is not the official TC-32:
  that one needs two people and stays `Not run` (#79, week 9).
